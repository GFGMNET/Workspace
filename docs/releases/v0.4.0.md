### New
- **Windows app, nothing to install:** download `project-workspace-v0.4.0-windows-x64.zip`, unzip it and double-click **ProjectWorkspace.exe**. Node is built in.
- **Help in the app:** *Help & wiki* in the sidebar, and a *Help* link on every feature that opens its page in the wiki.
- **Wiki:** a page for every feature with all its options.
- **Licence:** MIT.

### Fixed
- **VS Code tab:** it stayed blank because the browser blocked the embedded VS Code. It now shows through the app's own address, and no browser settings are needed (#30).

### Updating
- From v0.3.0 the app offers this update by itself. Click **Update now** in the bar at the top, or *v0.3.0 · Updates* at the bottom of the sidebar.
- If you used `start.bat` before, you can keep using it. Or switch to the exe: download the Windows zip, unzip it into a new folder, and copy your `data` folder over.
