# Vote for features

Ideas that are **not planned yet**. Show what you would like to see: open an idea and click **👍** (yes, please) or **👎** (not needed) on its first post. You need a free GitHub account; each person counts once, and you can change your vote any time.

**[→ All ideas, most wanted first](https://github.com/GFGMNET/Workspace/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3Avote+sort%3Areactions-%2B1-desc)**

| Idea | What it would do |
|---|---|
| [Skills from the internet](https://github.com/GFGMNET/Workspace/issues/118) | Paste the link of a skill (skills.rest, skills.sh, GitHub), see what it is, install it at your own risk |

Good to know:
- Only votes on the **first post** of an idea count.
- New ideas on this list come from the maintainer. Votes help decide what is planned next — they never change a release date.
- Found a bug? That is a normal issue with the label **bug**, not a vote.
