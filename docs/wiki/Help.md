# Help inside the app

The whole wiki is inside the app, also without internet.

- **Help** in the side bar opens the list of all pages, with a search box.
- The **(i)** button next to a feature's name shows a short line when you point at it; a click opens that feature's page in a side panel, so you can read it while you work.
- Links between pages stay in the app. Pictures are shipped with the app.

The pages are the same as on the [GitHub wiki](https://github.com/GFGMNET/Workspace/wiki) of the version you have installed.
