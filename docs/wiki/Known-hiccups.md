# Known hiccups

Small problems that are **not bugs** — for example something that goes away after a restart, or a setting on your PC.
Before you report something, look here first: maybe it is already known.

- Something that doesn't start? See the [[FAQ]] first.
- Found something new? Report it as a normal issue on GitHub.
